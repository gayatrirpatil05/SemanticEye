The Great Barrier Reef is the world's largest coral reef system, stretching over 2,300 kilometres along the coast of Queensland. I visited it last summer with my cousin, and the water was clearer than I had imagined. When oceans warm up, corals push out the algae that live inside them, and this makes them go white, which is known as bleaching. Furthermore, it is important to note that the preservation of marine ecosystems plays a crucial role in sustaining global biodiversity and economic stability. Tourism in the region provides work for tens of thousands of people, therefore looking after the reef is significant for the local economy and also for animals. Honestly, I think the saddest part is how slowly it recovers.
